Telly

Day-to-day help for a person with memory loss, with their family in the loop.

Telly runs in a phone or web browser. Alzheimer's care is the first focus.

What Telly does

  • Voice and camera help

    Ask by voice or text. Telly answers on screen and out loud, in more than one language. Point the camera at a medicine box, and Telly marks it on the picture.

  • Family alerts

    The family gets messages and health alerts, and can ask questions about the available health records. A missing signal shows as "unavailable", never as "all clear".

  • Medicine and meal check‑ins

    Reminders for medicine and meals, with check-ins that the family can see.

  • Reports

    Read lab results and prepare a lab report for the family to review.

Telly is a hackathon project, not a medical device. Alert limits are set by the family, not by a clinician. The screenshots show synthetic data.
