Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026.

Telly is a hackathon health assistant for a person with memory loss and their family. This page tells you what Telly stores and why.

Sign-in

Telly uses Google sign-in only to know who you are. Telly asks Google for the openid scope and gets a stable account ID. Telly does not read your Gmail, contacts, files, or calendar.

What Telly stores

When you use Telly, it stores the data that the app needs to work: your family group and its members, health readings and alerts, messages, reminders, medicine and meal check-ins, care notes, and shared locations if you turn location sharing on.

This data is in a SpacetimeDB Maincloud database. Lab report PDFs are in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Only members of your family group can see your family's data.

Some settings, such as phone numbers for calls, stay in your browser and do not go to the server.

Services that process requests

To answer a question, read a picture, or speak an answer, Telly sends the content of that request to the AI and voice services it uses, such as Google Gemini and ElevenLabs. If you connect lab records, Telly gets them through FinchNode.

What Telly does not do

Telly does not sell your data. Telly does not use your data for advertising.

Not a medical device

Telly is a hackathon project. It is not a medical device, and it does not give medical advice.

Contact

To ask a question, or to ask us to delete your data, write to vexzyl@pm.me.
